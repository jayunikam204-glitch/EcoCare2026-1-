🌱 What Is Plastic Pollution?

Plastic pollution happens when plastic waste harms our environment, oceans, animals and human health. We can reduce pollution by using less plastic and choosing reusable products.

🌊

Save Our Oceans

Keep plastic waste away from rivers and oceans.

🐢

Protect Wildlife

Protect animals from harmful plastic waste.

🌳

Keep Earth Clean

Dispose of waste properly and recycle whenever possible.

💡 How Can We Help?

  • Use reusable shopping bags.
  • Carry a reusable water bottle.
  • Avoid single-use plastic items.
  • Separate recyclable waste.
  • Spread awareness about plastic pollution.
